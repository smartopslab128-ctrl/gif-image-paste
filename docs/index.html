GIF & Image Paste

Collect GIFs and images, paste them anywhere with one click

Home FAQ Changelog Terms Privacy GitHub

GIF & Image Paste is a free, always-available Windows desktop utility for people who constantly reuse GIFs and images in chat, forums, email, and social apps. Keep a personal library on your PC, add items from the clipboard, files, URLs, or drag-and-drop, then click to copy or drag into another app.

About this project

This site is the official home for the app. The program is distributed as a portable executable for Windows 10 and 11. Source code and release binaries are published on GitHub so you can verify what you are running. The app stores your library locally on your machine; it does not require an account to use the core features.

Development is independent (“indie”). If the tool saves you time, optional support is welcome via Buy Me a Coffee — it is not required to use the software.

For questions, bug reports, and feature ideas, please use GitHub Issues. That is the fastest way to reach the maintainer.

Features

  • Add from anywhere — Clipboard (Ctrl+V), file picker, URL, or drag & drop
  • Custom tabs — Create your own tabs and assign items to them. Organize by project or category, like folders.
  • Favorites — Star items and filter by favorites for quick access.
  • Preview on/off — Toggle in the menu: click an item to open a large preview (with copy), or click to copy straight to the clipboard.
  • Paste anywhere — Click to copy, then Ctrl+V in Discord, Slack, emails, and more
  • Always available — Stays in the system tray; optional always-on-top window
  • Export — Save your library to a folder; quick export to the last-used folder
  • GIF-friendly — Animated GIFs play on hover; supports PNG, JPG, WebP, BMP

Who it is for

Community managers, moderators, streamers, support staff, and anyone who pastes the same reaction GIFs or reference images many times per day. The goal is to reduce friction: one small window next to your chat or browser, instant access to your library.

How it works

  1. Download the latest portable .exe from GitHub Releases and run it. The app can stay in your system tray.
  2. Add images via Paste, From file, From URL, or drag & drop into the window.
  3. Click an item to copy it to the clipboard, or drag the thumbnail into another application that accepts image drops.

Privacy & data (summary)

Your saved media and library metadata stay on your computer. The app may open network connections when you add an image from a URL, or when you open an external link (for example the project site or support page). Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

System requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
  • Portable executable — no installer required

Pricing

The app is fully free to use.

  • No item cap — Save as many GIFs/images as you need for your workflow.
  • No paid unlock — Organization features (tabs, favorites, export) are available to everyone.
